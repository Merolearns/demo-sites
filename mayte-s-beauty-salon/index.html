— Good to know

Before you book.

Every service lists its duration and price up front — and if it's not right, we make it right. That's our guarantee.

How much will it cost?

Most cuts & colour start at from $65 (sample starting price — the final price is confirmed at your consultation, before any work begins). Colour is quoted after we see your hair, so the price you approve is the price you pay.

How long will my appointment take?

A cut takes 30–60 minutes; colour and balayage take 90 minutes to 3 hours. Every service lists its duration and time, and your stylist confirms it at booking — no rushing, no surprises.

What if I don't like the result?

If it's not right, we make it right — re-cut, tone or adjust, no charge. That's our guarantee.

What to expect at your first visit

A real consultation first: your stylist looks at your hair, asks how you live with it, and agrees the cut or colour with you before touching a scissor. You leave knowing exactly how to keep the look alive at home.
