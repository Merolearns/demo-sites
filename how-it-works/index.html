What is included

  • Uptime and SSL monitoring — we watch the site for you
  • Backups, restore-tested
  • Monthly form + booking test, logged with date, destination and result
  • Security and dependency maintenance
  • 30 minutes of small edits a month (unused minutes do not roll over)
  • One-page monthly leads report, emailed to you
  • Next-business-day email support (a reply, not a fix on the spot)

What it does not include

So there are no surprises later, the plan does not cover: redesigns, new pages or sections, new features or integrations, copywriting projects, advertising, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed leads, or unlimited incident cleanup. Third-party subscriptions are billed separately.

What it costs without the plan

Honestly, very little: domain about $10–15 a year, hosting $0, SSL $0, DNS $0. The plan is labour, edits and monitoring — never hosting, because hosting costs us $0 and charging for it would be lock-in. You own everything either way.

The monthly report

One page, every month: what was checked, confirmed enquiries and bookings, comparison with last month, edits used, and one suggested next step. Phone and booking clicks are listed separately as interest — never counted as confirmed leads. It is built from data already collected free — Cloudflare visits and form submissions — so there is no dashboard to learn and nothing extra to buy. Each month also covers two small edits (inside your 30 minutes) and your review-request link and QR card, so new reviews keep coming.

What we will not promise

We never promise a number of customers. Nobody honest can. What we promise is the site, your domain, a test lead on your phone before the balance is due, and a monthly report that shows what actually came in.

We never invent a review, a price, or a result. Real reviews only, with the reviewer’s name and source. Real prices only, from your booking page. If a review or price is not real, it is not on the page.

The demo is a concept until you buy it. Every demo carries “Demo concept — not affiliated.” We never tell anyone the site is yours, live, or already working for you before you pay.

What if I already have a Facebook page / Google listing?

Keep them. A website does not replace them — it gives you something you own that shows up in Google and needs no login to view. We link the two together.

What if my nephew / cousin can build one?

If they have the time, that is a real option. The difference: this demo is already built — your photos, your reviews, your prices — and we can make it yours this week, with the booking flow working and the domain in your name.

What happens at handover?

A live 45–60 minute meeting, 48 hours after you receive the handover pack. You log in yourself and request a change. We confirm you own everything — domain, site, accounts — and our access is cut back to only what the care plan needs.
